Talos 24

A finance news channel built by AI, directly from the source. The live stream goes live soon.

What it is

Talos 24 follows what markets are discussing on X. Grok finds the posts, they are ranked by how many people engage with them, and promotional posts are left out. DeepSeek writes the analysis, an open-source voice reads it aloud, and the stream shows live prices and what prediction markets expect. The channel was built with Claude, Anthropic's AI. There are no human anchors.

Where to watch

On YouTube: youtube.com/@talosfinance24

The Talos 24 app

Talos 24 uses a Google sign-in, used only by the channel's owner, to upload the channel's videos and to read the channel's own YouTube Analytics. How it handles data is set out in the privacy policy.
