Talos 24 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy covers the Talos 24 app: the Google sign-in that the tools of the Talos 24 YouTube channel use. The app is used only by the channel's owner, for the channel's own account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in screen, the app uses:

  • The YouTube Data API, to upload the channel's videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read the channel's own videos and their public counts (views, likes, comments).
  • The YouTube Analytics API, read-only, to read reports on the channel's own videos, such as views, watch time and audience retention.

The app does not access any other Google data, and it does not access data about anyone other than the channel's owner and the channel's own videos. YouTube Analytics reports are aggregated: they do not identify individual viewers.

How the data is used

Only to publish and manage the channel's own videos, and to study how they perform so that future videos can be improved. The data is not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used for advertising.

Where the data is kept

The sign-in credential is kept on the owner's own computer and server, and is never published. Analytics reports the app reads are kept on the owner's own computer, for the owner's analysis.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data by the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Talos 24 YouTube channel, youtube.com/@talosfinance24.
